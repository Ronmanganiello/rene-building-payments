Add this app to your phone

It gets its own icon on your home screen and opens full screen, like any other app.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ (the square with an arrow). On newer iPhones, tap ⋯ first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Cafe Building from your home screen and sign in once. It stays signed in after that.
